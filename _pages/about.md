My name is 黄泓嘉 (Hongjia Huang / Wongka Wong), and I go by Alex. I am a master's student in the [MSR program](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/education/academic-programs/master-of-science-robotics/) at [Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/), working with Professor [Zackory Erickson](https://zackory.com/) in the [Robotic Caregiving and Human Interaction (RCHI) Lab](https://rchi-lab.github.io/). Previously, I earned my B.S. in **Computer Science** and **Mathematics** at [NYU Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/), graduating summa cum laude. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Professors [Furong Huang](https://furong-huang.com/), [Tianyi Zhou](https://tianyizhou.github.io/), and [Shengjie Wang](https://sheng-jie-wang.github.io/).

I build **physically grounded AI**: models that learn how the world moves and behaves, from molecular interactions to robot manipulation. I explore how models can represent geometry, motion, and physical interactions to better predict what happens and how to act.

At CMU, I have worked on training **vision-language-action models on Stretch**. My previous research explored geometric representations of motion for **3D world modeling**. I also worked on fine-tuning **geometric (3D) world-action models** and on **physics-informed vision-language-action models**, integrating 2D trajectories and 3D geometry into action prediction.

This interest in physical representations also extends to **molecular force fields**, where I worked on training models efficiently and exploring new representations of atomic environments.
